Architecture:
  C. Greeks 3D: per-contract Greek surfaces for Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta,
  Vanna, Charm and Rho; selectable Calls or Puts.
  D. Options Surface 3D: IV, Exposure (GEX/DEX/Vega/Theta/Vanna/Charm),
  Volume and Open Interest.
  X = strike moneyness relative to Spot (2.5 percentage-point buckets),
  Y = expiry/DTE, Z = selected metric. Primary renderer is a true Three.js
  WebGL mesh, restyled for QuantLab dark navy / teal / indigo / gold theme.

Data caveats:
  - At least two expiry rows and adjacent populated moneyness buckets are
    required to draw a surface; a single-expiry scope shows an explanatory state.
  - Missing cells are not zero-filled and faces are not drawn across gaps.
  - Greek surfaces show per-contract Greeks; exposure surfaces separately
    aggregate Greek × OI × multiplier.
  - Provider Greeks are preferred where available. BSM fallback uses r=0%,
    q=0.45% for QQQ and q=0% for GLD. Vanna, Charm and Rho are model-derived
    where provider fields are absent.
  - Binance OI does not identify dealer-side positioning; Volume is not signed
    buyer/seller flow. GEX Call+/Put− is a display convention, not verified
    dealer positioning.
