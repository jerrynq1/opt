SPOT FEED HOTFIX — INSTALL / VERIFY

What changed
1. The GLD/QQQ Spot price feed now uses Binance's documented raw stream:
   wss://nbstream.binance.com/equity/ws/price
   This is a separate WebSocket from the option-chain quote stream.
2. The option-chain stream uses its own connection and string RPC request IDs.
3. A rejected option-chain subscription no longer prevents the Spot socket from receiving prices.
4. The page now reports separate SPOT PRICE FEED and OPTION QUOTE STREAM diagnostics.
5. If no price snapshot arrives for 12 seconds, the raw price socket is closed and reconnected with backoff.
6. The Exposure renderer's undeclared `spot` reference has been corrected to `s.price`.
7. PWA cache version was bumped to v22.

How to verify after deployment
- Deploy the complete `opt-main` directory, including `sw.js` and `js/stock-options.js`.
- Fully close the installed iOS PWA and reopen it after the deployment is live. The service worker should update to cache v22; if it continues to show old assets, open the site in Safari once and reload, then reopen the PWA.
- In Options > Data Status, inspect `SPOT PRICE FEED`:
  * `Market closed · last available price` or `Market open` means a GLD/QQQ snapshot was received.
  * `Raw Price Stream connected · waiting for snapshot` means the socket opened but no snapshot was received yet.
  * `Đã nhận snapshot ... nhưng không có GLD/QQQ` means Binance sent a price snapshot, but the requested symbol was not present in its rates array.
  * `Price WebSocket mở nhưng không nhận snapshot trong 12 giây` means the socket opened but delivered no price payload; it will reconnect.
- Also inspect `OPTION QUOTE STREAM` independently. A Binance subscription rejection is now shown there rather than being hidden behind a generic Connected label.

Data policy retained
- OI null -> 0 and Volume null -> 0 for a received/accepted quote, with assumed-zero counters retained.
- A contract quote that has not been received is still unknown/incomplete; it is not zero-filled.
- No GEX, Max Pain, Gamma Flip or Greeks formulas were intentionally changed in this hotfix.

Validation
- `bash tests/run-all.sh`: 21/21 test files passed.
- JavaScript / MJS syntax: 58/58 files passed.
- The deployment's live Binance WebSocket connection could not be tested from the build environment. The Data Status diagnostics above are intended to distinguish transport failure from a snapshot that does not contain the requested symbol.
